\section{A coherent entropy--production compiler}
\label{app:graded}

This appendix proves Theorem~\ref{thm:coherent}. The bath source is used
to specify a finite bank; it is not an online supply. The two points that
permit coherent responses are a full response-label work estimate and a
simultaneous history-frame induction. Neither a forced-label scalar estimate
nor independence of the observer's histories would suffice.

\subsection{Compact source and full matrix work}
Restrict the source to its support. Write $k=\dim K$, $l=\dim L\leq rk$,
$\rho_g=B_g\rho B_g^*$, $S=S(\rho)$, and
\[
 \sigma_\mu=\sum_g\mu_g\rho_g,\quad a=S(\sigma_\mu)-S,
 \qquad \sigma=r^{-1}\sum_g\rho_g,\quad a_u=S(\sigma)-S.
\]
Here $\mu_g=\Tr(A_g^*A_g)/d$ is the actual Choi-input weight.
Minimizing the relative-entropy center gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weighted-uniform-gain}
 a_u\leq a/(r\mu_{\min}),\qquad \mu_{\min}\geq1/d.
\end{equation}
Indeed, evaluate the uniform center problem at $\sigma_\mu$ and use
$r^{-1}\leq\mu_g/(r\mu_{\min})$. The gap-selected version of
Lemma~\ref{lem:soft-source} gives a design source $\xi$ with
\begin{align}\label{eq:compiler-compact-source}
 \log_2 k'&\leq64(S+1)+16,& \log_2(1/p_{\min}(\xi))&\leq192(S+1)+51,\notag\\
 n a_\xi&\leq C_r\log_2(e(n+1))(1+na),&c_\xi&\leq9/16<3/4.
\end{align}
The filter changes half trace distance by at most $1/32$, so a Gram
entry changes by at most $1/16$. Selecting its threshold between source
square-root eigenvalues supplies the displayed spectral floor with no
second, horizon-dependent deletion. Common restrictions preserve every
isometry and multiplication triangle. These are offline design choices.
In the rest of the proof use this compact source and omit its subscript.

If $a_u\geq a_0=(512r\ln2)^{-1}$, an exact service with $n$ fresh pure
$r$-dimensional Stinespring cells and a charged clock costs $O_r(n+\log(n+1))$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:compiler-compact-source} bounds this by the claimed
quantity. Thus assume $a_u<a_0$ and $c\leq3/4$. The anchored triangle
identities imply that
$\sum_g A_g\otimes B_g^{\otimes t}$ is isometric for every integer $t$:
the off-diagonal column product in each gadget block is
$(B_1^*B_y-B_x^*B_z)/2$, and tensor powers preserve that equality.
This argument requires the untwisted table; it is not a rule for
arbitrary Kraus expansions.

Put $P=\rho^{\otimes t}$, $T_g=B_g^{\otimes t}$ and
\[
 \Sigma_t=r^{-1}\sum_g\rho_g^{\otimes t},\qquad
 R_t=(1-z)\Sigma_t+zI/l^t.
\]
In particular $\Sigma_t$ is not $\sigma^{\otimes t}$.
Concavity and subadditivity give $0\leq S(\Sigma_t)-tS\leq ta_u$.
For eigenvalues $p_i,q_j$ of $P,R_t$, define the label matrices, using
natural logarithms in the work variable,
\[
 M_\beta(g,h)=\sum_{ij}p_i\overline{T_{g,ji}}T_{h,ji}
 e^{\beta w_{ij}},\qquad w_{ij}=\ln(p_i/q_j),\quad D=M'_0.
\]
Each summand is a nonnegative multiple of a positive rank-one label
matrix. If $G_{gh}=\Tr(\rho B_g^*B_h)$ and
$\epsilon_0=(r-1)c^t$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full-work-moments}
 (1-\epsilon_0)I\leq M_0\leq(1+\epsilon_0)I,\qquad
 M_{-1}\leq(1+\epsilon_1)I,
 \quad \epsilon_1=(r-1)\gamma^t+(r-1)z,
\end{equation}
where $\gamma=c+2\sqrt{2r\ln2\,a_u}\leq7/8$.
For the second assertion, before regularization,
\[
 M_{-1}(g,h)=r^{-1}\sum_b[\Tr(\rho_b B_hB_g^*)]^t.
\]
Its diagonal is at most one. Pinsker applied to
$D_2(\rho_b\Vert\sigma)\leq ra_u$ bounds every off-diagonal summand
by $\gamma^t$, since $\Tr(\rho_gB_hB_g^*)=G_{gh}$.
Gershgorin gives the upper bound. The flat regularizer contributes a
positive matrix with trace at most $r$, giving the displayed $z$ term.
This is an operator estimate on all labels, not only their diagonal.

Direct summation and operator monotonicity of $\log$ give
\[
 \Tr D=r\{[S(\Sigma_t)-tS]\ln2+D_{\rm nat}(\Sigma_t\Vert R_t)\}
 \leq T_0:=rt a_u\ln2-r\ln(1-z).
\]
The scalar function $\phi(w)=w-1+e^{-w}$ is nonnegative; hence
$D-M_0+M_{-1}\succeq0$. With $e=\epsilon_0+\epsilon_1$ and
$m=T_0+(r-1)e$, the preceding lower bound and trace estimate yield
\begin{equation}\label{eq:work-variance}
 -eI\leq D\leq mI,\quad
 0\leq D-M_0+M_{-1}\leq(m+e)I,\quad
 M''_0\leq VI,
 \quad V=2(1+L)(m+e),
\end{equation}
where $L=\max\{t\ln(1/p_{\min}),t\ln l+\ln(1/z)\}$.
For $-L\leq w\leq L$, $w^2\leq2(1+L)\phi(w)$: for $w\leq0$
use $\phi(w)\geq w^2/2$; for $w\geq0$, the derivative of
$2(1+w)\phi(w)-w^2$ is $2w(1-e^{-w})$. Sum this scalar inequality
against the positive label matrices. The same procedure, using the
Taylor bound on $e^{\theta w}$, proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:work-mgf-gibbs}
 M_\theta\leq
 \exp\!\left(\epsilon_0+\theta m+
 \frac{\theta^2V}{2(1-\theta L/3)}\right)I,
 \qquad \sum_g T_gPT_g^*=r\Sigma_t\leq rR_t/(1-z).
\end{equation}
The first inequality holds for $0\leq\theta<3/L$. No operator
monotonicity of the exponential is used. The second inequality is the
incoming-flux certificate; its factor is $r$, not $r^t$.

\subsection{Finite integer code and incoming flux}
Set
\[
 \eta=z=\frac{\epsilon^2}{2^{20}r(n+1)^{12}},\quad
 x=\ln\frac{2^{16}(n+1)^8}{\epsilon^2},\quad
 t=\left\lceil\frac{\ln(8r/\eta)}{\ln(8/7)}\right\rceil.
\]
Then $\epsilon_0\leq\eta/8$, $\epsilon_1\leq r\eta$,
$m=O_r(ta_u+\eta)$, $V=O_r((1+L)(ta_u+\eta))$.
Write $h=1+L$, $x_+=x+n\epsilon_0$, and
\begin{align*}
 \theta&=\min\{(2h)^{-1},\sqrt{x_+/(nV)}\},\\
 B_+&=n(m+\eta)+2\sqrt{nVx_+}+2hx_+,
 &B_-&=n(\eta+\epsilon_1)+x.
\end{align*}
Alive energies are the integers $u=-\lceil B_-/\eta\rceil$
through $v=\lceil B_+/\eta\rceil$. Put $b=e^\eta$,
$s_i=\lceil-\ln p_i/\eta\rceil+8$,
$t_j=\lceil-\ln q_j/\eta\rceil+8$,
$E_{\min}=u-s_{\max}$, $E_{\max}=v+t_{\max}$,
$D_b=\eta^{-2}e^{-\eta E_{\min}}$, and $d_E=\lceil D_b b^E\rceil$.
Stock energies run from $u-s_{\max}$ through $v-s_{\min}$.
There are $N=E_{\max}-E_{\min}+1$ physical sectors.

Inside sector $E$, pack source coordinates $(i,E-s_i,a)$ of sizes
$d_{E-s_i}$. The ceiling margins ensure
\[
 e^{-10\eta}p_i\leq d_{E-s_i}/d_E\leq p_i,
 \qquad \sum_i d_{E-s_i}\leq d_E.
\]
Indeed the ceiling ratio differs from $b^{-s_i}$ by at most
$1+\eta^2$, since every stock count is at least $\eta^{-2}$.
Pack output coordinates analogously with $q_j,t_j$. Denote the
coordinate isometries by $C_p,C_q$ and the common input range by
$P_{\rm code}$. On that range set
\[
 \widehat B_g=C_q(T_g\otimes I_{\rm stock})C_p^*.
\]
All adjoint products are common compressions of $T_g^*T_h\otimes I$.
The coded round is therefore isometric on
$\mathbb C^d\otimes P_{\rm code}$ and can be completed to a unitary
on the full system and bank. The stock and its coordinates specify
matrix entries, not an additional retained tensor factor.

In every alive sector its normalized trace mean is
\[
 \tau_E(\widehat B_g^*e^{\beta\eta\mathsf E}\widehat B_h)
 =e^{\beta\eta E}M_E^\beta(g,h),\quad
 M_E^\beta=\sum_{ij}\frac{d_{E-s_i}}{d_E}
 \overline{T_{g,ji}}T_{h,ji}e^{\beta\eta(t_j-s_i)}.
\]
The summands remain positive label matrices. Thus
\begin{align}\label{eq:coded-matrix-means}
 \|M_E^0-I\|&\leq\epsilon_0+10\eta,\notag\\
 M_E^\theta&\leq\kappa_+I,
 &\kappa_+&=e^{\epsilon_0+\theta(m+\eta)+\theta^2V},\\
 M_E^{-1}&\leq\kappa_-I,
 &\kappa_-&=e^{\eta+\epsilon_1}.\notag
\end{align}
Means vanish outside the alive interval. The padding fraction is at
most $10\eta$, independently of off-diagonal means.

Amplify all sectors by the same dyadic factor $M$, providing a common
export port. With
$\Omega=\bigoplus_E(D_b b^E M)^{-1}I_{d_EM}$,
the virtual input Gibbs operator is bounded by $P$ tensored with the
stock Gibbs operator. Equation~\eqref{eq:work-mgf-gibbs}, output coding,
and $q_jb^{t_j}\leq b^9$ imply
\begin{equation}\label{eq:graded-inflow}
 \sum_g\widehat B_g\Omega\widehat B_g^*
 \leq\frac{rb^9}{1-\eta}\Omega,\qquad
 \sum_E\tau_E(P_E\Gamma_QP_E)\leq re^{12\eta},
 \quad\Gamma_Q=\sum_g\widehat B_g^*P_Q\widehat B_g.
\end{equation}
The input restriction commutes with the virtual Gibbs operator and
can only decrease it. Also $\|\Gamma_Q\|\leq r$. Consequently inflow
does not acquire a factor equal to the number of sectors.

\subsection{Concentration, reshaping and export}
Here is the concentration estimate used in the chronological argument.
For a frame $F:\mathbb C^m\to(\bigoplus_{p=1}^N\mathbb C^{D_p})
\otimes\mathbb C^s$, put $R=FF^*$,
$g=\|F^*F\|$, $\alpha=\max_p\|\Tr_{D_p}R_{pp}\|$,
and $D_{\min}=\min_pD_p$. For a Hermitian memory-only operator $A$
and independent block Haar mixers $V=\bigoplus_pV_p$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unequal-concentration}
 \Pr\{\|F^*[(V^*AV-\mathbb EV^*AV)\otimes I]F\|\geq s_0\}
 \leq2m\exp\left(-\frac{D_{\min}s_0^2}
 {2(1+N)\alpha g\|A\|^2}\right).
\end{equation}
For completeness, use Hermitian Hilbert--Schmidt orthonormal bases
$T_{p,a}$ and the product-group Laplacian
$\mathcal L=\sum_{p,a}D_p^{-1}\partial_{p,a}^2$.
Writing $X=V^*AV$, its centered diagonal and off-diagonal blocks all
have Laplacian $-2$. Therefore the centered compression $Z$ satisfies
$\mathcal LZ=-2Z$. The completeness identities
$\sum_aT_{p,a}YT_{p,a}=\Tr(P_pYP_p)P_p$ and
$\sum_aT_{p,a}^2=D_pP_p$ give
\[
 \sum_{p,a}D_p^{-1}(\partial_{p,a}Z)^2
 \leq \frac{2(1+N)\alpha\|A\|^2}{D_{\min}}F^*F.
\]
To see the $1+N$ factor, apply
$(XT-TX)R(XT-TX)^*\leq2XTRTX+2TXRXT$.
The two unscaled sums are bounded respectively by
$\alpha\|A\|^2I$ and $N\alpha\|A\|^2I$ after partial trace.
Haar integration by parts then bounds
$f(t)=\mathbb E\Tr e^{tZ}$ by $m e^{ct^2/4}$ with
$c=2(1+N)\alpha g\|A\|^2/D_{\min}$.
The required trace derivative inequality follows by diagonalizing $Z$
and integrating the scalar convexity inequality for the exponential.
Optimize exponential Markov at $t=2s_0/c$ and apply it to $-Z$ as well.
This proof explains both unequal-sector validity and the memory-only
restriction; it does not permit an operator on future history labels.

Reshape $F$ by $\widetilde F_{(a,h),s}=\overline{F_{(a,s),h}}$.
Its Gram is $\Tr_{\rm memory}R$, whereas its sector spectator marginal
is the transpose of $F_p^*F_p$. Hence $\widetilde g\leq N\alpha$
and $\widetilde\alpha\leq g$. Apply
\eqref{eq:unequal-concentration} to $\Gamma_Q^T$ and use
\eqref{eq:graded-inflow}. When $g\leq2$, the simultaneous inflow bound is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:graded-spectator-recursion}
 \alpha_{\rm call}\leq re^{12\eta}\alpha+
 2r\sqrt{N(1+N)\alpha\mathcal H/D_{\min}}.
\end{equation}
Each failure probability is at most $2s e^{-\mathcal H}$.
Conjugation and transpose here are algebraic reshaping, not physical
antiunitaries.

After a sector Haar mixer, export a common $e$-dimensional port and
replace it by a flat one. Column Grams and energy-weighted Grams are
preserved. The new spectator marginal has blocks given by Haar
conjugation of $I\otimes|u\rangle\langle v|$. Applying the single-sector
estimate to their Hermitian parts gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:graded-export}
 \alpha_{\rm next}\leq\alpha_{\rm call}/e^2+
 4\sqrt{2\alpha_{\rm call}\mathcal H/D_{\min}}.
\end{equation}
There are at most $2Ne^2$ tests. Explicitly, partial trace of the
retained register gives a factor $1/e$ from the flat import's purifier;
the mean contributes another $1/e$. The block-to-operator factor $e$
in the fluctuation cancels the first factor. These are exact partial
trace identities, not independence assumptions about frame entries.

\subsection{One simultaneous history-frame induction}
Initialize sector zero flat. Before each round and after each of the
first $n-1$ rounds choose block mixers offline, then fix them publicly.
After round $v<n$ replace
$j_v=\lfloor v\log_2r/2\rfloor-\lfloor(v-1)\log_2r/2\rfloor$
port qubits. There is no final replacement.
For analysis alone delete input padding after the premixer and output
energies outside the alive interval after the round. Let $F_v$ be the
surviving history frame and
\[
 G_v=F_v^*F_v,\qquad H_v^\beta=F_v^*e^{\beta\eta\mathsf E}F_v,
 \quad\beta=\theta,-1.
\]
Its columns are histories; the initial purifier, exports and import
purifiers are spectators, all used only in this dilation. Initially
$G_0=1$ and $\alpha_0\leq D_{\min}^{-1}$.

Impose concentration on all $r^2$ unweighted label blocks, both weighted
families, the padding projection, and each sector inflow. Use Hermitian
real and imaginary parts with threshold $\eta/(2r)$ so every full label
fluctuation is at most $\eta$. The Haar mean is a sum of
$M_E^\beta$ tensored with positive parent-sector Grams.
Thus the full matrix bounds~\eqref{eq:coded-matrix-means} imply,
assuming parent norm at most two,
\begin{align*}
 \|E_{\rm prep}\|&\leq21\eta,
 &\|G_{\rm call}-I_r\otimes G_{v-1}\|&\leq\delta_G:=2\epsilon_0+41\eta,\\
 H_{\rm call}^{\beta}&\leq\kappa_\beta I_r\otimes H_{v-1}^{\beta}+\eta I,
 &H_v^\beta&\leq(1+n\eta)\kappa_\beta^vI.
\end{align*}
Output cuts decrease positive weighted Grams; energy-preserving export
preserves them exactly. Markov in Loewner order at $B_-,B_+$ gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:graded-exit}
 \|E_{\rm exit}\|\leq\tau:=2(1+n\eta)e^{-x},\qquad
 \|G_v-I\|\leq v(\delta_G+\tau)<1/16.
\end{equation}
For the upper tail, if $\theta=\sqrt{x_+/(nV)}$, the variance term is
$x_+$ and the square-root buffer costs $2x_+$. In the other case put
$y=\sqrt{nV}/(2h\sqrt{x_+})\leq1$; the variance-minus-buffer part is
$x_+(y^2-2y-1)\leq-x_+$. In both cases
$n\epsilon_0-x_+=-x$. Integer endpoints only help. The lower tail is
$(1+n\eta)e^{-x}$. This also bounds every child before the output cut
by two, closing the induction.

Let $c_v=2^{2\lfloor v\log_2r/2\rfloor}r^{-v}\in(1/4,1]$.
Applying $\sqrt{u+c\sqrt u}\leq\sqrt u+c/2$ to
\eqref{eq:graded-spectator-recursion} and \eqref{eq:graded-export} gives
\[
 \sqrt{c_vD_{\min}\alpha_v}\leq
 e^{6\eta}\sqrt{c_{v-1}D_{\min}\alpha_{v-1}}
 +7\sqrt r(N+1)\sqrt{\mathcal H+1}.
\]
Consequently, for $v<n$,
\[
 \alpha_v\leq\mathcal B/D_{\min},\qquad
 \mathcal B=1024r(n+1)^2(N+1)^2(\mathcal H+1).
\]
The final round requires no spectator bound after it.

Here is an explicit, noncircular amplification. Set
\begin{align*}
 H_b&=\sum_Ed_E,\qquad e_*=2^{\lceil\log_2r/2\rceil},\\
 T_w&=\eta\max\{|E_{\min}|,|E_{\max}|\},\\
 Z_0&=1+\ln[128(n+1)N(r+1)^2e_*^2]+\ln H_b+n\ln r,\\
 K_0&=512r^2(n+1)(N+1)^2/\eta,\\
 A_0&=4+T_w+\ln K_0+Z_0,\\
 M&=2^{\lceil\log_2[4e^{T_w}K_0A_0^2]\rceil},\qquad
 \mathcal H=Z_0+\ln M.
\end{align*}
All quantities preceding $M$ are fixed without $M$.
Then $M\geq e_*$, $\mathcal H\leq3A_0$, and
\[
 D_{\min}\geq M\eta^{-2}\geq
 4r\sqrt{(1+N)\mathcal B\mathcal H}\,e^{T_w}/\eta.
\]
This follows, for example, from
$\sqrt{(1+N)\mathcal B\mathcal H}
\leq32\sqrt r(n+1)(N+1)^{3/2}(\mathcal H+1)$ and
$M\geq e^{T_w}K_0(\mathcal H+1)$.
Column count is at most $r^n$ and spectator dimension at most $H_bMr^n$.
There are at most $6r^2+N+1$ premixer tests and $2Ne_*^2$ export tests.
The tail bounds and a union bound make each successive good choice
exist with probability greater than $1/2$. Fix choices in chronological
order. The tested frames depend only on the responses and earlier
fixed matrices, never on an observer.

\subsection{Complete adaptive error and the resource ledger}
Purify an arbitrary adaptive, reference-assisted observer, including its
measurement records. Its ideal final dilation has history coefficients
$\psi_y$ with $\sum_y\|\psi_y\|^2=1$. The good physical dilation has
the same coefficients $\sum_y\psi_y\otimes F_n|y\rangle$:
all coded rounds have the canonical coefficients $A_g$, and all
other bank operations act only on the bath. At each analytical deletion
the removed vector has norm at most $\sqrt{21\eta}$ or $\sqrt\tau$.
The intervening physical circuit, retaining exports in the comparison
space, is isometric. Telescoping and the final polar isometry of $F_n$
therefore give vector error at most
\[
 n(\sqrt{21\eta}+\sqrt\tau)+n(\delta_G+\tau)<\epsilon.
\]
This does not suppose discarded branches never return. Half trace
distance of pure states is at most their vector distance, and partial
trace contracts it. Thus the same public circuit works for every
observer in the complete-experiment metric. No projection, history
register, runtime random seed or postselection is used by the device.

Encode the body into $\lceil\log_2H_b\rceil$ qubits, tensor the dyadic
port, and add a pure reversible clock. Outside padding is invariant
and initially empty. Precisely,
\begin{gather*}
 Q=\lceil\log_2H_b\rceil+\log_2M+\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil,
 \\
 P=Q-\log_2(d_0M)\leq Q,\quad C=0,
 \quad J=\lfloor(n-1)\log_2r/2\rfloor.
\end{gather*}
Every retained workspace is counted; in this unrestricted-unitary
specification model the fixed controlled matrices need no synthesis
workspace. A retained fixed service-wire padding costs only $O_d(1)$.
The physical initial source is flat on sector zero, not on the entire bank.

The total natural-log energy width is
$O_r(nta_u+(1+L)x+1)$, by $\sqrt{xy}\leq x+y$.
The body contributes this width plus $2\ln(1/\eta)+\ln N+O(1)$.
The amplification adds $T_w+\ln K_0+2\ln A_0+O(1)$;
in particular $n\ln r$ occurs inside $\ln A_0$, not as $n$ qubits.
Hence $Q\leq C_r[1+nta_u+(1+L)x]$.
For $y=\ln[128(n+1)/\epsilon]$, the compact-source bounds give
$t=O_r(y)$, $L\leq C_r y(S+1)$ and
$nta_u\leq C_{d,r}y^2(1+na)$.
Thus $Q\leq C_{d,r}y^2(1+S+na)$, proving the stated squared-log
bound. The selected spectral floor enters only the work-range parameter
$L$; concentration, chronological induction and the complete adaptive
error estimate above are unchanged. All constants are independent of
the original rank and spectrum.
