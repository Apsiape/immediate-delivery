\section{Source conditions in the Houghton smoothed family}
\label{app:houghton-sources}

The main text's cube-root service theorem concerns genuine group
devices. This appendix records the separate static results for
sources in the fixed smoothed geometry. Their full proofs are in
Appendices~\ref{app:houghton}, \ref{app:houghton-stability},
\ref{app:houghton-coercivity} and \ref{app:houghton-moderate}.

\subsection{The smoothed geometry with symmetric sources}

Genuine actions are not the only class for which the static exponent
can be fixed. Consider the existing constant-field smoothed corner
models of~\cite[Section~4.2]{DouglasMghirbiB}, with an $M\times M$
clock and $k$ exterior-algebra flavours on $L=4M+4$ one-particle modes.
Write $\Pi(W)=(\bigwedge W)^{\otimes k}$ for the collective action of
$U(L)$ on all flavours. This symmetry is a source hypothesis, not a
twirl that may be imposed without cost.

\begin{theorem}[Collectively invariant smoothed sources]\label{thm:collective-sources}
Let $M\geq12$, $k\geq1$. Suppose every normalized clock-diagonal source
block $\rho_c$ commutes with $\Pi(W)$ for every $W\in U(L)$.
Flavours and multiplicity spaces may be arbitrarily entangled;
off-diagonal clock blocks are arbitrary. Finite direct sums of these
models with arbitrary coherence between flags are also allowed.
For the resulting round, $u\leq1/16$ and $\ell\leq2/(15n)$ imply
\[
 S(\rho)+na(\rho)\geq c_Hn^{1/3}.
\]
A different zero-leakage exact-support round has target error at most
$1/16$ and cost at most $C_Hn^{1/3}$. The optimized leaky cost in this
class is $\Theta(n^{1/3})$, and concentrated replacement costs at most
a constant factor.
\end{theorem}

The number-sector sources give a useful concrete subclass. Write
$\tau_j=I_{\Lambda^j(\C^L)}/\binom Lj$.

\begin{corollary}[Sector-isotropic smoothed sources]\label{thm:number-sectors}
Let $M\geq12$, $k\geq1$ and let a source's clock pinching have the form
\[
 \Delta_c\rho=\sum_{c,\mathbf j}p(c,\mathbf j)\ket c\bra c
       \otimes\bigotimes_{l=1}^k\tau_{j_l}.
\]
The law $p$ may correlate clocks and all flavour degrees; off-diagonal
clock blocks and finite model-flag coherence are arbitrary.
These sources satisfy Theorem~\ref{thm:collective-sources}.
\end{corollary}

This is a static class comparison, not a lower bound on arbitrary
services. Entropy and production are measured before pinching.
Arbitrary noninvariant internal states, including diagonal ones, and
different round geometries remain unexcluded. The arbitrary-field
flux theorem for the fully flat fixed-region model already appears
in~\cite[Section~6.2]{DouglasMghirbiA}; this theorem concerns the source
extension of its constant-field geometry. Appendix~\ref{app:houghton}
gives both the concrete sector proof and the symmetry extension.

Full orbital invariance can be weakened, and the exponent is stable
under a small trace-distance perturbation without any lower bound on
source eigenvalues. Every one-particle link in this geometry fixes
$e_+=(1,\ldots,1)/\sqrt L$. Let $G_0=U(e_+^\perp)$, acting trivially
on $e_+$, and let $\mathcal E_0$ twirl each clock-diagonal block over
its collective action. Put
\[
 \delta_{\rm orb}(\rho)=\tfrac12
       \|\Delta_c\rho-\mathcal E_0(\Delta_c\rho)\|_1.
\]
Clock coherence is not constrained by this definition.

\begin{theorem}[Trace-stable symmetric-source exponent]\label{thm:houghton-stability}
In the same constant-field model, let $M\geq48$ and $k\geq1$.
For every source, possibly singular, the conditions
\[
 u\leq1/16,\quad \ell\leq2/(15n),\quad
 \delta_{\rm orb}(\rho)\leq\frac{\pi^2}{65536\cdot288}
\]
imply $S(\rho)+na(\rho)\geq4\cdot10^{-6}n^{1/3}$.
\end{theorem}

Appendix~\ref{app:houghton-stability} proves the entropy transfer and
the capped flux estimate needed here. The smaller group leaves an
inert mode in which pure-state coherence is allowed, so the class is
genuinely larger than full $U(L)$ invariance. The bound concerns this
fixed geometry, not arbitrary rounds. In particular low production
has not been shown to imply the required proximity uniformly as $M$
grows.

\subsection{Literal constant-field sources without imposed symmetry}

For the literal rational constant-field round, source entropy and
leakage can also be bounded without imposing orbital symmetry.
Every source below may be singular, coherent across clocks and
entangled across flavours. Entropy production is always the actual
gain $a=S(T\rho)-S(\rho)$ of the physical round, with
$T\rho=\sum_sw_sV_s\rho V_s^*$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:actual-dirichlet} compares this gain with the
Frobenius root energy of the actual slots.

The entropy estimate is uniform in flavour count:
\[
 S+1\geq c\min\{M,a^{-1/2}\},\qquad
 S+na+1\geq c\min\{M,n^{1/3}\}.
\]
It follows from exact chains of interior adjacent transpositions
(Theorem~\ref{thm:houghton-entropy}). The estimate saturates at the
clock size; a leakage lower bound must also force that size to grow.
The available conclusions are summarized in Table~\ref{tab:houghton-scope}.

The all-flavour bound uses a rational power $O(M^2)$ to expose a
fixed local $SU(4)$ group. With moderately many flavours, many
disjoint blocks and a small-angle contraction reduce that power to
$O(M^{3/2})$. Fixed and polylogarithmically growing $k$ meet the
moderate-flavour condition eventually, including the usual $k=2$
approximation family. At zero actual production, exact invariance
under round words allows the overlapping local groups to generate
a larger symmetry, with no bandwidth restriction. A bounded capped
flux then survives a fixed trace perturbation. Small gain alone has
not been shown to imply the required trace proximity.

The complete statements and proofs are in
Appendices~\ref{app:houghton-coercivity} and~\ref{app:houghton-moderate}.
At each fixed rational clock the cost is in fact $\Omega_M(n)$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:fixed-clock}); that argument alone gives no uniform
rate in $M$. For unrestricted flavours the growing-clock cost remains
between powers $1/4$ and $1/3$, up to logarithms. Optimized real fields,
alternative realizations and positive-production mixtures over clock
sizes are outside these conclusions. They are static source bounds,
not operational memory lower bounds over every device.
